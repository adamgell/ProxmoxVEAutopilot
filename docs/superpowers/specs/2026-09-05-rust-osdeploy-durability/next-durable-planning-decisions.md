### Bounded immutable-scope expiry scan

Use one separate in-memory keyset cursor over `(deadline_at, run_id, scope_key, operation_id)`, in that ascending order, with at most **32 operation candidates examined per sweep**. This is separate from the Waiting/Unknown repair cursor. Enumerate members through the existing closed stage-to-scope mapping and admitted registrations; do not derive scope membership from arbitrary strings or mutable scheduling rows.

Discovery is a read-only hint: select elapsed scopes with members in Pending/Leased/Running/Waiting/Cancelling or Unknown, excluding cancelled runs and members with an existing exact scope-expiry decision. Activated members use their original binding; genuinely unactivated members are eligible only for inherited scopes already opened by their real anchor, never an unopened mutation scope. Any malformed candidate still requires locked validation before action; the query alone is not proof of valid history.

Advance the hint to the last examined candidate even when locked validation rejects it, its state changed, or its original expiry was already adjudicated. A rejected candidate emits only a fixed bounded diagnostic/count, does not mutate history or get deleted, and cannot stop the rest of the batch. After reaching the end, reset the cursor for the next sweep; restart begins at the start. Newly due members sorting before the cursor are found after wrap. Fully adjudicated immutable history therefore cannot monopolize the first batch, and one invalid member cannot starve later members.

Each candidate transaction rechecks DB time, exact scope/anchor/binding, selected decision, cancellation, current authority and aggregate CAS under the existing full lock order. It chooses only the previously selected activated or genuinely unactivated expiry proof. Duplicate/restarted sweeps append at most one exact expiry decision. No cursor value conveys authority, extends a budget, creates a lease, repairs invalid history or promotes late evidence. Test more than 32 candidates, already adjudicated early scopes, an invalid early member, missing projections, cursor restart/wrap, and a member becoming due behind the cursor.

### Older opens cannot undo a timed-out close

Preserve the selected lifecycle Tokio mutex, short state mutex with open/in_flight only, Notify and watch Sender<bool>. Serialize awaited authority checks and drain under lifecycle. A synchronous fail-closed publication must not wait for that mutex: otherwise an earlier open holding lifecycle across a DB wait could reopen after close timed out.

- At first execution of open, pin its24second deadline and subscribe a watch receiver under state mutex before awaiting lifecycle. Retain that original unseen version throughout authority validation. Before opening, under the same state mutex, require has_changed()==Ok(false), zero activity, successful current owned-Rust-authority validation and unexpired original deadline. Invalidated work returns AdmissionClosed without refreshing its marker or retrying. Set open=true and publish false in that same critical section.
- At first execution of close, pin min(bound,24seconds), synchronously set open=false and unconditionally send_replace(true) under state mutex, including true-to-true closure, before awaiting lifecycle. After acquiring lifecycle, reassert closed and drain; register notification before checking activity. Never await while holding state mutex.
- Timeout while waiting for lifecycle or draining drops the owned inner future and synchronously republishes closure before returning TimedOut, without awaiting lifecycle. Authority stays unchanged. Timeout is not quiescence or handoff evidence.
- All marker-check/open and close publications share the state mutex. Never retain a watch value borrow while acquiring it. A genuinely new explicit open after close completion may freshly validate authority and open only at zero activity; an older call cannot promote itself into that new request. Order begins at async-body execution, not unpolled-future construction. Intervening watch publication may conservatively invalidate concurrent opens; all overlapping opens need not succeed. Bounds are cooperative, not hard real-time preemption guarantees.

Require deterministic bounded tests older_open_cannot_reopen_after_close_lock_timeout (real DB authority lock, closed startup,100msclose timeout, repeated true marker, old open still rejected after lock release); close_publishes_before_contended_lifecycle_wait (initially open, retained activity/private lifecycle holder, immediate rejection,100mstimeout, then release/join and successful subsequent drain); and queued_old_open_is_invalidated_but_fresh_explicit_open_can_succeed (two old marked opens remain invalid after close; only a fresh explicit request opens). Use private barriers/oneshots, select pause arrival against early worker completion, retain/join every worker, no guessed sleeps/public hooks/dependencies/clock changes. These supplement all existing Task8 cases.
